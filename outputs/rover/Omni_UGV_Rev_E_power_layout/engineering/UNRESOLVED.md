# Rev E — unresolved gates

Fabrication/production release is **BLOCKED**. Native ERC/DRC/parity are clear; physical function has not been tested.

1. **Primary-protector startup:** resolve BQ298217 cold-start and charger-wake compatibility with the 4.2 V BQ24074 output. A fully charged cell does not provide the documented typical 700 mV charger-detection margin. Test the complete state machine on a cell simulator before choosing a correction.
2. **Assembly process:** Q8/Q13 source-pad vias require accepted filled, copper-capped processing or further layout changes. See SOURCE_PAD_VIAS.csv. Review thermal-pad via tenting, stencil windows, solder voiding and missing custom 3D bodies. Retain the inherited J1 local hole-clearance exception until accepted by the fabricator.
3. **Power qualification:** establish actual board/current-path/via temperature rise, capacitor effective capacitance, regulator startup and Wi-Fi/camera transients. Scope stall, reverse, braking, USB insertion and both protection cutoffs. Regenerative current bypasses the charger; the primary charge-overcurrent threshold exceeds the assumed cell charge limit. A bulk capacitor alone is not a validated energy clamp.
4. **Cell and monitoring:** confirm the exact PowerHobby cell's charge/discharge/temperature limits, dimensions and harness ratings. Verify the 3.1 V switch's trip/recovery, off leakage, storage draw and body-diode charging. Validate both insulated, independently wired probes against measured cell temperature and the real NTC curve. The thermistors must be bonded to the cell, not placed on the PCB.
5. **Mechanical, camera and RF:** the existing CAD remains provisional. Measure motor tabs, shafts, couplings, wheels, battery and selected camera/flex; check brackets, fasteners and under-board clearance around added parts. Validate USB routing against the selected stackup and validate camera orientation, rails and timing. Test RF with the solid perimeter and assembled battery/brackets. Carpet performance remains an experiment.
6. **Firmware/Vita and sourcing:** implement default-disabled arming, command timeout, ramps/limits, battery/temperature/fault monitoring and measured wheel signs. Verify Vita networking and decoding before choosing the streaming protocol. Lock the remaining passive MPNs and approved supply chain before release. No working firmware or qualified mechanical assembly is delivered by this six-step electrical revision.

The next engineering gate is a reviewed solution for item 1, followed by assembly-process acceptance and a current-limited bring-up plan. No order or publication is authorized.
